production conclusions about slice count. N12 is provisional: the experiment
uses 96 positive-weight incoming and 98 outgoing directions, compared with
production's 1,920 incoming and 4,374 outgoing directions. Visible banding and
near-rim reference errors remain unresolved; their angular/spatial causes have
not been isolated. The latest Union Glacier pose has local integration checks,
not a fresh independent-probe accuracy qualification.

The experimental 3,036 versus production 1,049 spatial nodes do not demonstrate
a threefold cost for adding the dimension at production settings: existing
horizontal sampling also differs. Keeping production sampling and adding a
dense 12-slice axis would nominally multiply field storage by 12 before valid
domain pruning, roughly 3.1 GiB per production-resolution field. The actual
ragged allocation, build cost and required count are unmeasured.

approach. A time-varying lookup/camera adapter and independent phase checks are
not implemented. Source radius/height changes are separate physical changes.

These are retained questions, not an active execution queue. No production
