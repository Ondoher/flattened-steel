## Objective

Adapt the standard San Jose box scene and its shadow lighting to the GPU-resident distant-Sun animation renderer, beginning with a single 55-degree-FOV view two hours before sunset aimed toward the sunset position.

The subsequent creative continuation follows the owner-authored animation
prompt and delivers a full astronomical day at a desert observatory. The
experiment proves the complete animation-and-recording path at native 4K;
near-horizon rendering corrections and local-Sun animation are separate work.
