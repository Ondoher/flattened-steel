## Admitted Domain And Cost

Reuse applies to the pinned homogeneous flat atmosphere and black physical
ground, isotropic local source, fixed source height 4,828,032 m, source-centered
cylinder radius 33,378,000 m and altitude 0-100,000 m. Every consumer must retain
complete interpolation and ray support. Exact source/observer placement and
observer-centered viewing dome remain live frame inputs. Height, medium,
emission, ground, numerical or coverage-policy changes require compatible
replacement construction; unsupported queries reject rather than clamp.

The compact field has 1,049 positions. Six wholly empty bands receive neither
storage nor scattering evaluation; 55 bands are admitted. The matched omission
control appended 132 proof-only empty slots. Its measured 99.1 MiB peak-field
saving and 72,028,440 avoided scalar writes are relative to that control, not
historical production-grid savings. The compact layout costs more than the old
285-position grid. Its retained cumulative field is 275,299,560 bytes (262.5 MiB),
with peak live construction fields 825,898,680 bytes and a separate 33,592,320-byte
phase payload. Host readbacks and driver/JS overhead are separate.

The owner accepts the observed 30-36-second startup cost in the [plan](plan.md#p5--qualify-defensibility-motion-and-benefit).
The priority is avoiding rebuilds. Current P3-D poses have bit-identical complete
fields and paired PNGs; this supports reuse within the declared symmetry,
not unbounded placement or different physical assumptions.
