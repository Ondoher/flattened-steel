## Ordered Work

### Q0: Authenticate And Register

- Opening is established; retain the captured opening baseline and declare the
  coherent source-review unit before source work. Freeze selected compiler/backend and input
  packets; demonstrate parity with the unchanged current construction control.
- Reproduce the H33 phase/history witnesses from qualified inputs. Add the
  20-degree upper control and source-facing/cross-Sun/away-Sun headings so a
  horizon repair cannot hide angular damage elsewhere.
- Register exact candidate direction/weight inventories, reference refinements,
  absolute floors, predictions and resource estimates before candidate evaluation.
  Declare nonnested grids honestly; do not fit weights to benchmark residuals.
- Publish an early baseline visual using the [visual-progress contract](design.md#reviewable-visual-progress),
  before starting the longer candidate construction work.

### Q1: Qualify The Angular Calculation Cheaply

- Evaluate constant incident radiance and independent smooth/directional/grazing
  fields across the stored outgoing grid and representative noon, low-Sun and
  boundary states. Check positive weights, total solid angle, physical-frame
  parity and both phase integrals. Respect real ground/visibility discontinuities.
- Screen at most three systematically constructed positive rules. Prefer a
  fixed-rule replacement compatible with the existing transported-radiance field.
  Outgoing-dependent importance sampling is a separately scoped architecture
  alternative if fixed rules prove too costly, not an implicit compiler rewrite.
- Use independent boundary/phase-resolving integration with refinement. Initial
  engineering targets: at most 0.5% phase-mass error over tested outgoing states
  and at most 1% physical-field/source error above the registered absolute floor,
  with reference refinement below one quarter of the allowance. Report all misses
  and near-zero absolute errors. These targets qualify this screen, not N4.
- Select the least expensive passing rule. Preserve species histories and angular
  regions; a correct scalar normalization alone does not establish source accuracy.
- Update the comparison sheet after the angular screen, showing the measured
  phase/source changes and available spectral color comparisons.

### Q2: Propagate A Matched Candidate Through N4

- Run a timed one-wavelength, one-state control/candidate pilot first. Measure
  intermediate-field sizes, closure/support expansion, GPU time and throughput.
  Sparse queried rays do not guarantee sparse N4 construction; account for the
  full dependency closure. Extrapolate before launching the next allocation.
- Regenerate pure J1-J4 with the selected incoming rule. Transport each corrected
  order into the next; frozen old higher-order increments cannot qualify the new
  total. Capture pure-order and cumulative spectra at matched low/upper rays.
- Verify J1 invariance, per-order CPU/GPU/frame parity where used, physical units,
  positivity allowance and one independent refinement. Freeze the same view and
  reconstruction operations for baseline and candidate. Retain compact spectra;
  stream wavelengths and clean owned transient fields after dependent evidence.
- Compare propagated source and ray spectra with existing qualified independent
  references where compatible. Label unavailable higher-order references explicitly.
- Publish the first complete spectral witness comparison as soon as available;
  update it at meaningful order/candidate milestones rather than waiting for the
  entire benchmark. A one-wavelength pilot uses labelled scalar previews.

### Q3: Score Accuracy And Locate Cancellation

- Authenticate the paper measurement reference, preprocessing and aggregation,
  freeze the current algorithm baseline and selected published/reproduced paper
  comparison scores before candidate evaluation. The established
  benchmark is 17 times x 81 directions x 40 transported channels, scoring 0-30;
  use its actual spectral interpretation rather than relabeling Native15 probes.
  Full-cache acceptance requires matching or improving paper RMSE; an improvement
  only against libRadtran does not satisfy that gate. Qualify libRadtran separately
  for isolated source/order/component tests with matched physical inputs.
- Report baseline/candidate spectral RMSE plus signed errors by wavelength,
  elevation, Sun angle and time. Retain separate horizon images under identical
  display mapping as visual evidence, not an accuracy target.
- Join matched order/species/angular deltas into a cancellation map. Evaluate
  deltaSSE = 2 residual dot delta + norm(delta)^2. Distinguish actual numerical
  error cancellation from opposing contributions or redistribution across sky.
- If the angular correction exposes another bias, identify and independently
  qualify that calculation before a baseline/A/B/A+B factorial. Freeze factor B
  and its prediction before new candidate outcomes. Do not simultaneously change
  density normalization, physical aerosol parameters, cache resolution and view
  sampling to obtain a favorable aggregate score.

### Q4: Decide And Hand Off

- Publish numerical-convergence, isolated reference-agreement, full paper RMSE,
  horizon and construction cost results separately. A mathematically improved
  source with worse paper RMSE is diagnostic evidence and fails full-cache
  acceptance. Investigate the compensating calculation and combined correction
  while preserving the paper atmosphere; do not qualify the candidate from its
  isolated libRadtran gain alone.
- Decide whether to retain the candidate, revise the rule, or propose a separate
  correction for the newly exposed bias. Production promotion requires its own
  production-owned adaptation and verification; this experiment does not update
  Flat32 defaults or replace operational cache assets.
- Local-Sun applicability needs its own reproduced deficit. Its selected N1
  rendering does not acquire higher-order angular work merely because the
  distant-Sun N4 construction changes.
