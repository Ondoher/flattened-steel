# Caches

The selected strobing promotion's [release preparation](distant-sun-strobing-release.md)
owns the proposed package/default selection and compatible rollback.

Focus: Cache construction, storage, reconstruction, loading, and optimization.

Current work: [distant-Sun strobing promotion](distant-sun-strobing-promotion-plan.md),
with progress and next actions in its
[status](distant-sun-strobing-promotion-status.md). Existing accepted cache and
compiler qualifications below remain baseline evidence for this new work.

The shared-block distant-Sun cache is implemented, qualified, and adopted
through the complete P1-P8 promotion plan. Its contained storage, generator, transport,
residency, packet-rendering, animation, and final-audit experiments are closed.
D0 and D1 select the production architecture, and P1 implements the production
format, packet-mapping, runtime-publication, and rollout-response contracts.
P2 shared-inventory generation is implemented and
[qualified against all 109 controls](shared-generation-qualification.md).
P3 browser transport and Worker preparation is implemented and
[checked at its production boundaries](shared-transport-verification.md).
P4 renderer-owned packet-atlas primitives are implemented and
[checked through exact GPU probes](shared-atlas-verification.md).
P5 residency and atomic publication are implemented and
[checked against exact metadata and ownership boundaries](shared-residency-verification.md).
P6 shader and stable live-runtime integration are implemented and
[checked at the shader and publication boundaries](shared-runtime-verification.md).
[P7 combined qualification](shared-runtime-qualification.md) is accepted for
bounded discrete time selection on the declared target;
[P8 publication, rollback observation, acceptance, and bridge removal](../../../../archives/topics/production-algorithm32-release-20260911/agents/topics/production-algorithm32/caches/shared-runtime-rollout.md)
are complete. Historical POC, P0-P10, and PA0-PA7
execution remains archived and is not reopened.

The [point-local compiler promotion](../../../../archives/topics/production-algorithm32-release-20260911/agents/topics/production-algorithm32/caches/point-local-compiler-promotion-plan.md) is
also closed. Production now has one generalized compiler, exact Native15 cache
payload compatibility, authenticated Direct40 reconstruction, and an official
Q30 Bruneton B1 reproduction through current production GPU transport.
