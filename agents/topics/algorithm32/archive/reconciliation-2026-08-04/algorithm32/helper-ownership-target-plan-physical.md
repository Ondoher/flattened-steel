## Decision Contract

- A non-construction `keep-on-class` row retains its current path, class
  symbol, and callable form. A construction-shaped `keep-on-class` row retains
  its semantic class owner and path while Step 4.5.5 decides whether its
  construction facade remains.
- A non-construction `return-to-class` row names its exact class method.
- A construction-shaped row fixes its semantic owner and path now but leaves
  method, constructor, direct value, or removal form to Step 4.5.5.
- An F-batch keeps a deferred construction entry and its inseparable helpers
  together; Step 4.5.4 must not create a temporary wrapper, export, or partial
  owner seam.
- Every namespace module has one named namespace export. No compatibility
  re-export, barrel, duplicate implementation, or single-method namespace is
  planned.
- `remove-unused` is the explicit retirement action for the sole zero-consumer
  row, `sourcePathLimitDistanceToMeters`.

The canonical 95-candidate construction ledger includes four
`keep-on-class` rows: `LocalSunConstructionDomainSelection.fromGeometry` and
the three `LocalSunCpuFields.create*` statics. All four retain their semantic
class owner here but use `defer-construction-form-to-4.5.5`; Step 4.5.5 may
retain or remove a static construction facade without reopening that ownership
decision.
