source demonstrates equivalent contracts across owner boundaries:

`CubedSphereDirectionDomain._clamp`,
`SpectralCalculator._multiplySpectralValues`, and

output, evaluation, and unit semantics, not purity or naming. All other
foundation class operations remain on their classes.

for `CubedSphereDirectionDomain` or `SphericalNativePositionDomain`, or
spectral-basis and spectral-packet construction/acceptance operations whose
value invariants belong to `SpectralBasis` or `SpectralPacket`. Step 4.5.5
still owns the later factory-retention decision.

No row remains genuinely ambiguous after applying the class-responsibility and
demonstrated cross-boundary-contract tests.
