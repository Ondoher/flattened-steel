# Production MVP Goals

Focus: Independently trackable feature and technical outcomes for the first
production MVP, including completed capabilities and remaining acceptance work.

The [goal list](requirements.md) owns status, acceptance, evidence and
dependencies. F01–F07 describe selected app capabilities; T01–T13 describe
technical, numerical, release, research and distribution outcomes. IDs are
stable references, not execution order or phases.

[Scene objects and content selection](requirements.md#f08) are required for
MVP. Boxes, generated terrain, and actual geographic data with satellite
imagery remain options to investigate, in increasing complexity and decreasing
priority. At least one content option must be selected and delivered; all three
are not required. The chooser design and supported modes remain open.

Several app capabilities and retained-update/recovery goals are complete.
Animation playback and several acceptance goals remain partial or open. Status
includes the working tree; it does not claim a fully qualified release build.

Add owner-selected goals as scope evolves using the next unused identifier
and the [extension rules](requirements.md#reading-and-extending-the-list).
Unselected ideas remain proposals. Scope/claim reductions must be recorded
explicitly rather than silently dropping an open requirement.

The [former-requirement mapping](requirements.md#mapping-from-the-former-grouped-requirements)
preserves existing obligations and incoming M1–M3 links without keeping phases.
The [research register](../../../../references/followup.md#color-reset-research-register)
continues to own detailed source and official-experiment rows.

## Context boundary

Load this entry and the goal list for MVP assessment or scope changes. Follow
the selected goal's evidence/dependencies when planning work; do not reload
every originating plan. Standards follow affected paths through the
[manifest](../../standards/manifest.md). Engineering reviewers remain cancelled;
this reorganization selects no implementation or new review workflow.
